Process

Project as a systemstep by step

Each step reinforces the next: planning sets the logic, concept defines the character, visualization locks the aesthetic, drawings make it real.

One rhythm, one logic, one aesthetic thread — from the first plan to the final specification.

The average timeline for a full design project is around 3 months, depending on area, scope and approval speed.

Planning

The foundation. We align wishes, ergonomics, daily scenarios and technical limits. Planning defines furniture composition, light logic and everyday comfort.

brief options approval

Concept

Concept sets the mood: materials, textures, color, form plasticity and light character. Moodboards help to quickly approve the direction before visualization.

mood palette materials

Visualizations

Photorealistic rendering of each room. Angles show the atmosphere exactly as it will be in reality.

camera light details

Drawings

The technical manual for the builders. Exact layouts of electrics, plumbing, finishes and custom furniture nodes.

nodes plans specs

Procurement

Detailed specification with products, vendors and pricing — from finishes to decor. Custom pieces can be designed when needed.

vendors budget delivery

Custom furniture

We sketch built-ins and custom items, select veneers, enamels, and approve prototypes with carpenters.

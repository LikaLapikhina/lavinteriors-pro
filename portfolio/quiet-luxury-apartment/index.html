Quiet Luxuryapartment

A residence where restraint becomes the loudest statement. No ornament for ornament's sake — only material, proportion and light doing what they do best. Calacatta marble, smoked oak, aged brass: each surface chosen for its silence, not its spectacle.

Key design decisions

  • Open living zone with kitchen island — removed two walls to achieve a single breathing space
  • Three-layer lighting: architectural recesses, mid-level pendants, floor lamps — no ceiling spots
  • Floor-to-ceiling joinery hides storage without breaking the visual plane
  • Palette anchored in warm stone and muted linen — the artwork is the only accent
  • Custom furniture: sofa and dining table designed specifically for these proportions
